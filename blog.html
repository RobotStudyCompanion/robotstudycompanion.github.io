The Robot Study Companion took part in IEEE RO-MAN 2025, bringing together members of the RSC team for the robot design competition and research presentations.

Farnaz Baksh presented at the Robots for Humans workshop, while Farnaz Baksh and Matevž Zorec represented the RSC team in the robot design competition.

The team received the Best Technical Design Award.

RSC v4 brings together lessons learned from previous prototypes with a focus on affordability, repairability and open hardware.

The build documentation is available through the RSC Docs page.

The RSC Summer School introduces students to human-robot interaction and social robotics through hands-on design, programming and prototyping.

Students work in multidisciplinary groups to develop robotic applications addressing real-world challenges.

The Robot Study Companion is developed as an open-source hardware platform, allowing students and researchers to inspect, modify and build upon the system.

Students have contributed to the RSC through theses, software development, hardware work, testing and documentation.

The RSC project has involved collaboration with students and researchers in Guyana, including work with the University of Guyana.
