🤖 Designing Social Robots for Human Interaction

The intersection of humans and robots has become increasingly critical as society integrates robotics into everyday life. This course offers an in-depth exploration of social robotics, a branch of robotics that involves the design, development, and study of robots that interact and communicate with humans or other robots in a socially meaningful manner. Participants will investigate concepts such as robot design for social interaction, ethical considerations, and real-world applications using and prototyping various robot platforms as hands-on case studies.

📌 General Information

  • Credits: 3 ECTS (Pass/Fail)
  • Language: English
  • Level: Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD students
  • Field: Robotics, Artificial Intelligence

🧩 Requirements

  • Basic programming skills (Python, Git, Bash, etc.)
  • Familiarity with robotics concepts (recommended)
  • Interest in AI, robotics, and interdisciplinary applications

🎯 Learning Outcomes

  • Understand key concepts in human-robot interaction
  • Design and evaluate socially interactive robots
  • Program and customise robotic systems
  • Apply AI, MLOps, and IoT concepts
  • Assess ethical and societal implications
  • Develop and present a working robot prototype

📖 Recommended readings:

  • Chapter 2, 3, 4 & 11 https://www.human-robot-interaction.org/
  • Guy Hoffman and Xuan Zhao. 2020. A Primer for Conducting Experiments in Human–Robot Interaction. (March 2021), https://doi.org/10.1145/3412374
